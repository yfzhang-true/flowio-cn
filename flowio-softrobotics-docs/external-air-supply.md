# External Air Supply

> Source: https://www.softrobotics.io/external-air-supply

You also have the option to use an external air source (up to 6 bar pressure) instead of a pump module! Simply take the Main module and connect your external air supply to the inlet valve on the right-hand side. For example, you can connect an external compressed air tank or a CO2 tank pressurized up to 6bar. Yes, it's that simple! And you don't even need to change anything in the software!
